# Subgroup

A subgroup $H$ is a subset of a [[group]] $G$ that forms a group under the same operation.

The even integers form a subgroup of the integers under addition: they contain 0 and are closed under addition and taking negatives.

A [[cyclic-group|generated subgroup]] collects powers of one element. A [[coset]] translates a subgroup by an element of $G$.

[Source: MIT, Introduction to Groups](https://symm4ml.mit.edu/symm4ml_s26/notes/intro-to-groups).
