# Coset

Given a [[subgroup]] $H$ of $G$, the left coset $gH$ is $\{gh : h \in H\}$.

Under addition modulo 6, let $H=\{0,2,4\}$. Its distinct cosets are $H$ and $1+H=\{1,3,5\}$.

Cosets partition $G$, and each has the same size as $H$. These facts give [[lagranges-theorem|Lagrange's theorem]].
